# Farmart Storefront

Online grocery storefront catalog and daily promotional deals.

## Language

**Product**:
An item available for purchase in the grocery catalog with price, inventory, and category metadata.
_Avoid_: Goods, item, SKU

**Category**:
A grocery classification grouping related products (e.g., Fruits & Vegetables, Frozen Seafoods).
_Avoid_: Department, taxonomy, collection

**Brand**:
The manufacturer or label associated with a set of products.
_Avoid_: Vendor, maker

**Top Saver**:
A time-sensitive promotional offer displaying inventory progress and discount countdown.
_Avoid_: Flash sale, daily deal, bargain

**Cart**:
A customer's pending collection of selected products prior to order placement.
_Avoid_: Basket, shopping bag
