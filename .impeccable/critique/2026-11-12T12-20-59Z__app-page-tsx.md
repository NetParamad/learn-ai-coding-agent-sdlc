#### Design Specificity Verdict

**Verdict: Category-interchangeable marketplace template (not authored for Farmart grocery).**

- **LLM assessment**: Structural sameness mirroring a multi-vendor marketplace template rather than a trusted, fresh grocery store. Critical lack of grocery-specific trust markers (farm origins, freshness guarantees, dietary/organic badges).
- **Deterministic scan**: CLI scan on rendered DOM reported 16 warnings, 2 advisories: `low-contrast` (2.4:1 on `#fff8e6`), `tiny-text` (11px body text), `overused-font` (100% Geist), and `nested-cards`. Crucially, headless browser inspection caught the severe `text-amber-400` contrast failure on light gradient (1.58:1 ratio) in `hero-banners.tsx`.
- **Visual overlays**: Browser evidence captured in Light Mode confirms severe headline wash-out, placeholder vector icons replacing food photography, and an intrusive 3-field registration form embedded inside the deal grid.

#### Overall Impression
The storefront has clean structural bones and responsive card grids, but light mode suffers from a critical contrast failure in the hero headline, reliance on sterile vector icons instead of fresh grocery photography, and an account registration card awkwardly jammed into the product deals feed.

#### What's Working
1. **Responsive Spatial Scaffolding**: 12-column grid layout, tactile card corner radiuses (`rounded-2xl`), and well-proportioned container gutters.
2. **Header Utility & Cart Transparency**: Prominent category-scoped search bar, 24/7 phone hotline reassurance, and explicit cart trigger with item badge and live balance total.
3. **Product Card Micro-Architecture**: Orderly hierarchy of badges, brand, title, star ratings, and dual strikethrough pricing.
